# P02 · Escalation handoff

Version 1

## When to escalate

Escalate uncertain high-impact cases, unresolved conflicts, and clusters with evidence spanning several customers or merchants. Record the focal transaction and list related observations by evidence ID.

## Handoff

State the suspected pattern, what the investigation verified, and what remains open. Use scoped references so the next analyst can inspect the evidence. Escalation preserves the case for human review.
