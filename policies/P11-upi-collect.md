# P11 · UPI collect requests

Version 1

## Payment direction

A collect request asks the payer to authorize a debit. Inspect the request direction, recipient history, amounts, and repeated requests. A familiar merchant invoice can be legitimate. A new requester plus unusual or repeated debit attempts merits closer review.

## Scope

The synthetic corpus models historical collect flows. When intent cannot be established from the available observations, escalate with the exact context required.
