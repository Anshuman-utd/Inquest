# P03 · Account takeover

Version 1

## Evidence

Compare the device, category, amount, and time to the customer's prior behavior. A device change with familiar spending can indicate normal replacement. A device change combined with unusual activity and a novel recipient is stronger evidence.

## SIM re-registration

Treat recent UPI re-registration as an observed signal. Check whether unusual transfers followed and whether recipients were previously seen. A SIM or handset change can be legitimate. Escalate when recipient or registration context is incomplete.
