# P06 · Travel and location mismatch

Version 1

## Reliable locations

Compare elapsed time and distance between reliably observed physical points. Account for accuracy and location source. Remote checkout may be associated with a merchant or network location far from the customer.

## Travel exception

An away-from-home purchase can fit a plausible trip, especially with familiar device use or a coherent sequence of locations. Escalate conflicting location evidence when the decision depends on it.
