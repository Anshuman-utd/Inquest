# P09 · Mule and cash-out activity

Version 1

## Recipient concentration

Look for UPI-style transfers from several customers to the same recipient in a short window, especially following unusual account activity. Cite distinct senders, time window, and the recipient's observed history.

## Alternatives

Fundraisers, group payments, and popular recipients can create similar concentrations. Escalate clusters when the data supports a linked pattern but offers limited context about the recipient.
